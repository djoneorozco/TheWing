The Story behind, Data Science

Commitment to Service

As a retired combat veteran, I built TheWing.ai to continue serving the military community through technology. My background in engineering management at Johns Hopkins and quantitative management and business analytics at Duke shapes how I approach that mission: understand the problem, examine the data, and build something useful.

TheWing brings that approach to life through military decision tools and Amy, our AI guide. My goal is to help service members and their families understand their options, explore the tradeoffs, and make informed decisions about what comes next.
